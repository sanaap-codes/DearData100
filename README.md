# DearData100
Creative Coding - Dear Data 100 Assignment

Topic: What People Do On The Train
Everyday, hundreds of people travel by train, each spending that time differently. Some passengers keep themselves entertained using phones, books or have conversations, while others sit quietly or close their eyes.

I have decided to record this data data and observe what passengers on trains do. This includes whether they are sitting or standing, and the activities they carry out, such as using their phone, talking, eating, reading a book, sleeping and sitting quietly. I am collecting this data during different times of the day; in the morning, on my way to University and in the afternoon, on my way home.

Progress:
So far, I have recorded a total of 44 data items from the morning and afternoon of 20 October, which contains information about people's activities and whether they are sitting or standing. I am also currently organizing my data across different journeys, but the layout may change later on.
